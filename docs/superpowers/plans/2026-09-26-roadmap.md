# Gujarat Restricted-Items Authorisation Platform — Development Roadmap

**Spec:** [`highlevel_plan.md`](../../../highlevel_plan.md) and
[`2026-09-28-licence-applications-and-demo-design.md`](../specs/2026-09-28-licence-applications-and-demo-design.md)
(licence types, applications and renewals, Demo milestone)

This roadmap splits the work into separately executable plans. Each one ends with working, tested
software. A phase is planned in detail only when the one before it is merged and its entry
criteria are met, so no plan is written against guesses.

**Order:** Phase 1 → **Demo milestone** → review with the authorities → Phases 2–7. The
authorities' feedback may change Phases 2–7, and those phases absorb whatever the demo already built.

## Product priorities

1. **Secure and compliant**: this is not negotiable (DPDP Act 2023, Gujarat Prohibition Act).
2. **Very user friendly**: a "What's next" card for every role, step-by-step wizards with drafts saved automatically, plain-language requirements with examples, status timelines like a parcel tracker, error messages that say how to fix the problem, WCAG 2.1 AA, and works at phone width.
3. **Strong, compelling demo**: set up with one command, runs offline, uses only synthetic data, and can be reset between rehearsals.
4. **Simple, testable, easy to interpret code.**

## Engineering principles (apply to every phase)

**Simple, testable, easy to interpret**
- Modular monolith: one Django app per module (`identity`, `licensing`, `positions`, `catalogue`,
  `applications`, `documents`, `inventory`, `transactions`, `approvals`, `transport`, `audit`,
  `compliance`, `demo`), plus `core`. Modules call each other only through a small `service.py`
  of plain functions.
- Business rules are plain functions that can be unit-tested without HTTP, for example
  `resolve_rule(licence_type, substance)`, `trading_permitted(licence, at)` and
  `resolve_approval_chain(area, severity, qty)`. Views stay thin.
- Test-driven, against real Postgres, running as the restricted `gj_app` role. The demo journeys are
  also tested end-to-end in a browser with Playwright.
- One responsibility per file, a module docstring explaining why the file exists, type hints on
  public functions, no metaprogramming.
- Frontend: React, TypeScript and Vite with an accessible component library. All text goes through
  i18n files (English now, Gujarati later).

**Secure and compliant by construction**
- The app connects as `gj_app` (not the owner, `NOBYPASSRLS`, no DDL); migrations run as
  `gj_owner`. Every business table gets its RLS policies in the migration that creates it.
- Append-only records (audit, transactions, decisions, application revisions, licence validity
  periods, rule versions) are enforced with `REVOKE UPDATE, DELETE` plus a trigger.
- Identifiers and contacts are encrypted at the field level, with a blind index for exact-match
  lookup. Documents are encrypted by the application, scanned with ClamAV, and stored in India.
- Deny by default, with RLS as the second, independent check. Every read of another party's
  sensitive data is audited.
- CI gates every merge: lint, tests, dependency audit, `check --deploy`, and a migrations check.
- Demo features only work with `DEMO_MODE=1`, and the app refuses to start if demo mode is combined
  with production settings.

## Phase 1: Foundation (detailed plan ready)

Scaffold, CI, least-privilege DB roles, field encryption, user and role model (`LICENSEE`,
`APPLICANT`, `PERSONNEL`, `LICENSING_AUTHORITY`, `SOFTWARE_OWNER`, `HEAD_AUTHORITY`), RLS context,
hash-chained audit log, OTP, password and OTP login with lockout and rate limits.

**Exit criteria:** CI green; `verify_audit_chain` passes; `check --deploy` clean; the bootstrap
Software Owner can log in with password and OTP.

## Demo milestone

**Spec:** section 8 of the 2026-09-28 design. Production-quality thin slices of:
- **Licence application journey:** register as an applicant, then the guided checklist, then
  submit with an OTP. The verifier returns it, the applicant resubmits, the verifier forwards it,
  and the Licensing Authority approves. The licence is issued with its permissions card.
- **Transaction journey:** the seller looks up the buyer by licence number and enters the item,
  quantity and transporter details. Permission and limit checks run with plain-language blocking
  messages. The buyer confirms with an OTP, then the Area Officer and District Officer approve.
  Every step shows on the status timeline.
- **Underneath:** areas, positions and assignments; licence records with type, scope and frozen
  permissions; licence-gated enrolment; demo catalogue and rules; document store (local disk)
  with ClamAV; a seeded read-only stock list; a demo approval policy matrix; reason codes.
- **Demo tooling:** `make demo` (offline Docker Compose), `seed_demo`/`reset_demo` (synthetic
  data: state-code-99 GSTINs, dummy phone numbers, PDFs watermarked "SYNTHETIC — DEMO"), a persona
  picker, a Demo SMS inbox drawer, and `docs/demo/script.md`.

**Exit criteria:** both journeys pass the Playwright test that follows the demo script; a
`reset_demo` rehearsal works end-to-end offline; a security review of the branch is clean.

## Phase 2: Licensing & positions

**Entry criteria:** the demo review is done; OTP channel and an India-resident SMS/email provider
chosen; Gujarat area levels and codes mapped to the excise and police structure; CSV import format.

**Scope:** harden and complete what the demo built. Licence records (type, scope, permissions
snapshot, validity periods, suspend/revoke), `trading_permitted()`, manual entry and bulk CSV
import, forgot-password, session listing and revocation, position transfers with history, and
notifications.

## Phase 3: Licence applications & renewals

**Entry criteria:** real licence types, permissions and requirement lists from the Licensing
Authority (they replace the demo placeholders); legal position on grace periods (currently 0).

**Scope:** complete what the demo built. On-screen catalogue and rule-version management, all
five requirement kinds, both review modes, the return limit and lapsing, withdrawal, renewals and
the renewal window, the 7-day clean-up of unused uploads, and the production document store in
an India region.

## Phase 4: Inventory

**Entry criteria:** severity and complexity classes agreed with the Licensing Authority.

**Scope:** per-licensee inventory, capped by the licence's `max_stock_qty`, and visible only to
its owner.

## Phase 5: Transactions, transport & approvals

**Entry criteria:** the approval policy matrix and the reason-code list.

**Scope:** complete what the demo built. The full state machine, cancellation, a Transport licence
check for transporters, the policy engine across severity × area × quantity, and Software Owner or
Head Authority provisioning of personnel accounts.

## Phase 6: Oversight & compliance

**Entry criteria:** a named legal reviewer; retention periods for each data category.

**Scope:** Head Authority dashboards and exports (edit rights to be defined), anchoring the audit
log to a WORM store, a list of `COMBINED` rules, the vacancy list, DPDP consent, retention and
data-principal requests, and application and document retention.

## Phase 7: Hardening & go-live

Threat model, penetration test, load test, backup and restore drills (database and documents),
key rotation, HSTS preload, ClamAV and object lock in production, deployment to an India region,
and legal sign-off.
